# Guide: IT Setup for New Starters

Welcome to Orbital Logistics IT operations. This guide covers your first two days.

## Day one

1. Collect your laptop and building badge from it-support. Bring photo ID.
2. Sign in and set up multi-factor authentication with the authenticator app. You cannot reach any internal system without it.
3. Install the VPN profile from the self-service portal, and check that you can open the operations portal from outside the office network.

## Day two

- Request access to your team's systems through the access portal. Your manager approves the request, and so does each system's owning team.
- Join your team's channel, and the incident announcements channel.
- Read the on-call handbook and the incident communication policy.

## Before your first on-call week

New engineers shadow an experienced engineer for one full on-call week before they join the rotation. During the shadow week you receive the same pages, but the experienced engineer stays responsible.

If something on this list doesn't work, open a P4 ticket for it-support.
