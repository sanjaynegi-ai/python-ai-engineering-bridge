# Guide: Suspected Phishing

Phishing emails try to make employees reveal passwords or install malware, often by imitating a login page.

## If you receive a suspicious email

Do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail client, which forwards the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox.

## If someone clicked a link

1. Report it to the security team immediately, even if the person did not type anything.
2. If a password was entered, reset it at once, and sign the account out of all sessions.
3. The security team checks the sign-in logs for the account, and decides whether the incident needs further investigation.

Clicking a phishing link is treated as a security incident, not as a mistake to hide. Quick reporting limits the damage.
